About this site

USA State Shape Quiz is a free browser game that helps anyone learn U.S. geography by matching state outlines to state names.

How to play

  1. Start a round of 10 questions randomly selected from all 50 states.
  2. Look at the state outline and choose one of four names.
  3. Get instant feedback, a short fact, and a U.S. locator map highlighting the state.
  4. Tap Next under the choices when you are ready for the next question.
  5. See your final score, streak, and accuracy — then play again anytime.

Data sources

State boundary shapes are generated from datasets/geo-admin1-us, which is based on Natural Earth data. Natural Earth data is dedicated to the public domain. The packaged dataset uses the Open Data Commons Public Domain Dedication and License (PDDL).

Educational facts are short summary statements intended for casual learning. They are not an official government publication.

Advertising

This site is prepared for future advertising (for example Google AdSense). Ad placeholders are shown only outside the answer buttons so they do not interfere with gameplay. Ad code is not active yet.
